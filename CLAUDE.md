# CLAUDE.md — Evriel Systems website

This repo is evrielsystems.com (React + Vite + GSAP, deployed on Vercel).

A FULL REBUILD is planned and approved. Before any design or build work,
read REBUILD-PLAN.md in the repo root: it is the single source of truth for
the new design system, sitemap, page-by-page specs, asset rules and build
order. Follow it exactly.

Non-negotiable rules from the plan:

- Build ONE page at a time, finished and approved by Bereket before the next
- White Apple-style base; deep teal #012624 only for logo, link hover, small labels
- Each project has its own color (table in REBUILD-PLAN.md part 2)
- Real product screenshots and screen-recorded videos only; never AI images
  for products that exist
- CLIENT RESPECT: no client prices, stock figures, customer or staff names,
  or internal emails may appear in any screenshot or video
- Never use em dashes in site copy
- Site copy is plain, simple English

Older files CLAUDE-CODE-FINAL-COMMAND.md and EVRIEL-DESIGN-REFERENCE.md
describe the OLD site and are superseded by REBUILD-PLAN.md.
